Frequently asked questions

Is Primez free?

Yes. Courses, notes, study spaces and the leaderboard are free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start as a guest, and your progress and notes are saved in this browser. Create an account to keep them across devices and to use friends, study spaces and the leaderboard.

How do I share a note?

Open a note in your library and choose Share. You can turn on a read-only link, post the note into a study space, or copy it as Markdown. You can turn the link off at any time.

What is a study space?

A private group for up to 20 people. Members join with an invite code and share a weekly XP board, a chat and notes.

Which AI tools do the courses cover?

There are dedicated courses on ChatGPT and Claude, plus lessons on choosing between assistants, prompting, agents and safety that apply to any AI tool.
